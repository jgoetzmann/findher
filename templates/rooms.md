# Two rooms, and why

Every fact here is marked VERIFIED or UNVERIFIED. VERIFIED means it was read off
the organiser's own page, not an aggregator, and read this week.

Every row carries a `why`. The rule the preflight enforces: the reason must
still make sense with any demographic word deleted, so no demographic word may
appear in the row at all. A recommendation you cannot justify without naming the
category is targeting, not taste, and the justification language is the tell.

`kind` is `density` or `survivability`. Exactly one of each. The metric that
predicts the outcome is the fourth visit to the same thing, so being visible in
eight places is being invisible in eight places.
