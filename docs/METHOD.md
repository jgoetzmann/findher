# The method

## Two rooms. Refuse the third and the fourth.

The metric that predicts the outcome is showing up to the *same* thing a fourth
time. Being visible in eight places is being invisible in eight places.

So: one room picked for density, one picked for survivability, and the second
one decides it. The question is not whether the room is full of the right
people. It is whether you could go a fourth time without it becoming a chore.

If the two axes do not intersect, the plan reports the gap and stays at one
room. It does not invent the room that would have satisfied both.

## Where the outcome actually comes from

| Factor | Share | Who does it |
|---|---|---|
| Rooms entered | ~35% | you, on a Wednesday |
| Initiating | ~25% | you |
| Company kept | ~20% | you |
| Appearance | ~15% | a threshold, cleared once |
| Follow-up | ~5% | you |

Sixty percent of it is walking in and speaking first. No tool does either.

## Ten standing rules

1. Same room, four times, before judging the room.
2. Speak first, or the visit did not happen.
3. One question, not two. Two makes the reply a form.
4. Leave an exit ramp on every ask. An ask without one is a demand.
5. Never report back what you observed about someone. That is the tell.
6. No comment on appearance, ever, in any direction.
7. The first message names the shared room, or it could have gone to anybody.
8. Do not move platform before there is a reply.
9. Log the room, never the person.
10. Stop at two rooms.

## Appearance

A threshold good, not a linear one. All of the value is in clearing the floor
and none of it is above it. Four photographs, taken by another person, is the
whole of it.

**The costume rule**: every recommendation must be justifiable without naming
the group you hope it appeals to. If the justification needs the group, the
recommendation is a costume, and the justification language is the tell —
because that is the language that leaks into how you talk about the room once
you are in it.

## The anti-patterns

| Pattern | What it looks like | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Breadth | Eight events, one visit each | Nobody remembers a first-timer |
| The audit | Reciting your own criteria at someone | Your seed is a shopping list; read aloud it is an audit |
| The dossier | Knowing things about them you were not told | Fine for research, disqualifying for a person |
| The optimisation | Rewriting the profile a ninth time | Below the threshold nothing helps, above it nothing helps |
| The wait | Building more plan instead of going | The criterion is a row in a log, for exactly this reason |
